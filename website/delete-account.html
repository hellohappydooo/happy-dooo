Account and Data Deletion

Under Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, users have the right to request deletion of their account and associated data directly through the app or through this web form.

⚠️ What Happens When You Request Deletion:

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, registered mobile number, email, saved delivery addresses, order history, and preferences from our active databases within 30 days. Financial audit records required by law will be securely anonymized.

How to Delete Your Account:

Method 1: Directly inside the Happido Mobile App

  1. Open the Happido app on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to Profile / Account Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and select "Delete My Account".
  4. Confirm your request via OTP verification. Your account will be immediately queued for deletion.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, you can request account deletion by emailing our support team:

  • Send an email to: support@happido.in
  • Use the subject: "Happido Account Deletion Request"
  • Include your registered Mobile Number and Email Address.
