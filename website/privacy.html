Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective: November 7, 2026

Welcome to Happido ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how Happido collects, uses, discloses, and protects your information when you use our mobile applications (Happido Customer - com.mshappido.customer, Happido Partner - com.mshappido.restaurant, and Happido Rider - com.mshappido.rider) and our related online services.

We are fully committed to protecting your personal data and complying with Google Play Developer Program Policies, GDPR, and Indian Information Technology laws.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information from and about users of our applications:

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, email address, and delivery address provided during registration or checkout.
  • Location Data:
    • Approximate and Precise Location: When you permit location access, we collect foreground location data to pinpoint your delivery address, display nearby restaurants, and calculate accurate delivery fees.
    • Background Location (Rider App Only): The Happido Rider app collects background location data exclusively during active delivery shifts to enable turn-by-turn routing and provide live order tracking to customers, even when the app is minimized.
  • Transaction & Payment Data: Payment details processed securely by licensed third-party payment gateways (Stripe / Razorpay). Happido does not store your full debit/credit card numbers.
  • Device and Usage Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, and crash reports.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To process, fulfill, and deliver your food and grocery orders.
  • To provide accurate real-time tracking of assigned delivery partners.
  • To verify accounts and prevent fraudulent activities or misuse.
  • To notify you about order updates, service alerts, and customer support resolutions.
  • To improve app stability and user experience through analytics and error diagnosis.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only in the following contexts:

  • With Delivery Partners (Riders): Your name, delivery address, and delivery instructions are shared with assigned riders solely for the purpose of completing delivery.
  • With Merchant Partners (Restaurants): Order item details and special cooking instructions are shared to prepare your order.
  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting infrastructure (AWS / GCP), SMS OTP verification gateways, and payment processors.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures, including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, strict database access controls, and token-based authentication to safeguard your information.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active. You have the right to request deletion of your account and personal data at any time.

To request permanent account and data deletion, visit our dedicated Account Deletion Page or email us at support@happido.in with the subject "Delete My Account". We will remove your profile and personal identifiable information within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Our applications are designed for general audiences and are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 18 where required by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our Data Protection Officer:
